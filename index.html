★ Hidden Gem
Turkish ~10 min
Tucked away and completely worth finding. Bosphorus brings genuine Turkish hospitality to Cary — the Adana kebab is the move, but everything on the menu is carefully made. End the meal with Turkish tea and you'll understand why this is a local secret.

Explore Cary & Raleigh

From live entertainment to outdoor escapes — this area has more going on than most people realize.

Discount for hotel guests
Live Entertainment
Koka Booth Amphitheatre
★ Must See
Outdoor Concert Venue ~8 min · 8003 Regency Pkwy
Set among 14 acres of hardwoods and pines next to Symphony Lake, Koka Booth is one of the best outdoor venues in the Southeast. Major touring acts, NC Symphony performances, food festivals, and July 4th fireworks. Check the calendar — there's often something on when you're here.
Visit website
Lenovo Center
Arena · Concerts · Hockey ~20 min · Raleigh
Home of the Carolina Hurricanes and host to the biggest touring acts in the country. If there's a major show or an NHL game during your stay, this is where it's happening. One of the better arena experiences you'll find on the East Coast.
Directions
The Cary Theater
★ Local Gem
Indie Cinema · Live Events ~10 min · Downtown Cary
A 1946 landmark beautifully restored and still running strong. This isn't a multiplex — expect hard-to-find documentaries, classic film screenings, local comedy nights, and live performances. Completely worth a visit if you want something different. Check their calendar online.
Directions
Health & Wellness
Augment Wellness
★ Hidden Gem
Cold Plunge · Recovery · Biohacking Outside your front door
One of the most impressive wellness studios in the entire Triangle — and it's literally next door. Augment is a cold plunge and biohacking studio built around recovery, performance, and mental clarity. You'll find cold immersion tubs, infrared sauna, red light therapy, compression therapy, hyperbaric oxygen, BrainTap meditation, salt therapy, and more under one roof. Whether you're a road warrior who wants to reset after travel or someone who takes recovery seriously, this is a rare find. Book a session before you leave.

Dine-In Cinema ~8 min
Full dinner and cocktails delivered to your reserved recliner seat while the movie plays. An elevated moviegoing experience — a step well above a standard theater. Book seats in advance online and arrive 30 minutes early to order food before the film starts.
Directions
Page-Walker Arts & History Center
★ Hidden Gem
History · Art Gallery ~10 min · Downtown Cary
Housed in a beautifully restored 1868 hotel building, this free gallery showcases Cary's history through artifacts and photographs from the railroad era through today. Small and intimate — takes about an hour — and almost no one outside the area knows to go. Completely worth it if you're curious.
Directions
Nature & Outdoors
Hemlock Bluffs Nature Preserve
★ Hidden Gem
Nature Preserve · Trails ~10 min
140 acres of protected forest featuring rare Eastern Hemlock trees — a species almost completely absent from North Carolina's Piedmont. Three miles of quiet, well-maintained trails. No crowds, no noise. If you have an hour to decompress, spend it here. Completely free.
Directions
Bond Lake · Fred G. Bond Metro Park
Park · Boating · Fishing ~10 min
Rent a paddleboat or kayak on Bond Lake, fish from the banks, or just walk the trail that wraps around the water. One of the most peaceful spots in Cary — and few people outside the area know about it.
Directions
Downtown Cary Park
Park · Events · Live Music ~10 min
Completely reimagined and reopened in 2023 after two decades. Seven acres with a great lawn, pavilion, feature fountain, and a packed calendar of free events — live music, outdoor movies, fitness classes, and art exhibitions most evenings. Check what's on while you're here.
